# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Ultimate Cheatsheet for Developers welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds. Be respectful and constructive in project spaces so everyone can participate safely.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat others with courtesy and assume good faith.
- Give helpful feedback about ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Respect different experiences, viewpoints, and boundaries.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, personal insults, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces run by the project, as well as when someone represents Ultimate Cheatsheet for Developers in public.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@zlatanstajic.com](mailto:contact@zlatanstajic.com). Include relevant links or context, but do not post sensitive details publicly. The maintainer will review reports as confidentially as practical, consider the safety of everyone involved, and respond as soon as practical.

Depending on the circumstances, the maintainer may ask for a change in behavior, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant from project spaces. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not tolerated.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
